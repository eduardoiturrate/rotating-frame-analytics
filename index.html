Rotating Frame analytics

A private desktop tool used by one person, the owner of the TikTok account @rotatingframe, to read the statistics of that same account (views, likes, comments and shares of its own videos) for a weekly report that guides which educational science videos to make next.

It has no other users, posts nothing and shares nothing. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Channel: How Space Works and How Spin Works, educational videos about orbits and rotation.
